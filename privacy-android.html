Save the Fleet privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · BeitSolutions LLC / Balaji Elankovan

This policy describes Save the Fleet for Android and its Google Play purchases.

No game account or advertising

You can play without creating a game account. The Android game does not include advertising, advertising identifiers, social chat, user-uploaded content, or enabled analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not use game data for advertising or behavioral profiling.

Information on your device

Mission progress, stars, selected themes, sound preferences, tutorial completion, and cached content settings are stored on your device. They are not uploaded to a game account. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes locally stored progress and preferences. Purchases remain associated with your Google Account and can be restored.

Google Play purchases

Optional theme and level purchases use Google Play Billing. Google processes payment and account information under its own policies. The game receives product information and signed purchase records to check ownership, restore unlocks, and acknowledge completed purchases. These records are processed on your device; they are not sent to a BeitSolutions purchase server. We do not receive your full payment-card details. Purchases on other platforms do not transfer.

Content settings and technical requests

The game requests built-in theme and level availability settings over HTTPS from balaelan.com. As with a web request, the server and our hosting provider receive your IP address and standard request details, such as the endpoint, time, and app user-agent. Hosting services may record these details in operational or security logs. They are used to deliver and protect the service, not to track you across apps or target advertising. We do not intentionally derive a player's location from these requests.

Service providers and sharing

Google Play operates the store and payment services. Our hosting provider, HostGator, delivers content settings. Technical information necessary to provide these services may be processed by those providers. We do not sell player data. Information may be disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the service.

Children and families

Save the Fleet is intended for players aged 6–17 and can be played without providing a name, email address, birth date, contacts, photos, microphone access, or precise location. The game does not ask children to create an account or communicate with other users. Parents and guardians should manage purchases through Google Play's family and purchase-approval settings. A parent or guardian should contact support on a child's behalf and avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Retention and privacy requests

Local game data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall. Google retains its own account and transaction records under its policies. Hosting logs are maintained for service operation and security in accordance with the hosting configuration; they are not used to create a permanent player profile. If you contact support, the information you voluntarily send is used to address your request. You may contact us to ask about technical-log retention or request review or deletion of information we control, subject to necessary security and legal retention.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact beitsolutionsllc@gmail.com. Do not post personal information in public GitHub support requests.

Changes

We may update this policy when game features or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
