Save the Fleet privacy policy

Updated November 16, 2026 · BeitSolutions LLC / Balaji Elankovan

This policy covers Save the Fleet on iOS and Android, including older versions still installed on players’ devices.

Current free edition

The free edition (iOS 3.8 build 39 and Android 3.6 build 6) includes all six themes and ten levels. It does not start the game’s remote settings service or store billing service. Normal gameplay does not upload progress or preferences to us.

Local game information

Progress, completed missions, stars, selected theme and level, sound settings, and tutorial completion are stored on the device. We do not operate game accounts or a cloud progress service. You can remove local data by clearing app storage on Android or deleting the app on iOS. Device or operating-system backups may retain a copy under your platform settings.

No advertising or tracking

The audited game has no advertising, cross-app tracking, social chat, or enabled developer analytics or crash-reporting service. It does not request your name, birth date, contacts, photos, microphone, or location. We do not sell player data or use it for targeted advertising.

Older versions and technical requests

Earlier versions request theme and level settings from balaelan.com over HTTPS. The server and hosting provider receive an IP address and standard request information, including request time, endpoint and user-agent. Such information may be retained in hosting operational and security logs to deliver and protect the service. The game does not use these requests to infer your location, build advertising profiles or upload gameplay progress. The current free edition disables this automatic request.

Earlier store transactions

Earlier editions supported optional store transactions. Apple or Google handled payment and account information. The game checked store-supplied ownership records on the device; it did not send those records to a BeitSolutions transaction server. We do not receive full payment-card details. The current free edition does not initialize this service. Apple and Google retain their own store records under their respective policies.

Support and external pages

If you choose to contact support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you send, and use them to respond to your request. Please do not send sensitive information or post personal information in public GitHub issues. Support and privacy links open external websites. GitHub Pages and our website host receive normal web request information when you visit these pages; these visits are separate from normal gameplay.

Service providers and security

HostGator hosts the legacy settings endpoint and balaelan.com. GitHub Pages hosts the support website. These providers process technical requests to operate and secure their services. Settings requests use HTTPS. We do not share player information for advertising. We may disclose information where legally required or necessary to protect the service.

Children and families

The game is intended for ages 6–17 and can be played without submitting personal information or creating an account. Parents and guardians should contact support on a child’s behalf and avoid including unnecessary personal information.

Retention and deletion

Local game information remains on the device until removed through app or device storage controls. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and necessary security or legal obligations. Hosting log retention depends on the provider and hosting configuration; we do not promise a fixed deletion period that has not been verified. To request access to or deletion of information we control, email beitsolutionsllc@gmail.com with the subject “Save the Fleet privacy request”. Tell us what information you want removed. For a support message, use the same email address you originally contacted us from; do not send passwords or payment details. We review requests for support correspondence and identifiable technical records. We may need limited information to locate a request and may retain information required for security or legal reasons. Store records and device backups are controlled separately by Apple or Google.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email beitsolutionsllc@gmail.com. We update this policy when our practices change and show the revision date above.
