Need a hand, captain?

Help for Save the Fleet on iPhone and iPad. Find answers below or send a bug report or question to the developer.

Contact support

Support requests use GitHub and require a GitHub account. Requests are public: please do not include your email, phone number, passwords, or other personal information. Younger players should ask a parent or guardian to contact support.

Report a problem

Include the app version, your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, theme, mission number, and steps that reproduce the issue. A screenshot of the game can help.

Share an idea

Tell us what would make your next fleet adventure better. Use the support form for suggestions about controls, themes, routes, or sound.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Protect the moored fleet by placing guards beside the waterways to stop incoming enemy ships. Earn coins to add defenses and progress through missions.

How do I place a guard?

Drag a guard from the guard dock onto a valid spot beside the water. Try a different spot if placement is unavailable, and check that you have enough coins.

Can I play in portrait or landscape?

Version 3.3 supports both orientations. Rotate your device. If it stays in portrait, check the orientation lock in Control Center.

How do I pause or change sound?

Use the in-game pause and sound controls. If audio is missing, check the game's sound setting and your device volume, silent mode, and connected headphones.

The game froze or closed. What should I do?

Close and reopen the app, then check for an update in the App Store or TestFlight. If the problem repeats, send the steps and mission number through the support form.

How do I update the TestFlight version?

Open TestFlight, select Save the Fleet, and tap Update when a new build is available. TestFlight builds expire; use the App Store release when it becomes available.
